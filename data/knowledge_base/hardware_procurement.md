# IT Hardware Procurement, Refresh & Replacement Policy

## Overview
This policy details the standards for allocating, upgrading, replacing, and returning IT equipment provided to full-time and contract staff.

## Standard Equipment Bundles

### 1. Engineering / Data Science Track
- **Laptop:** MacBook Pro 16-inch (M3 Max, 36GB Unified Memory, 1TB SSD) or Dell XPS 15 (Intel Core i9, 32GB RAM, 1TB SSD, Ubuntu certified).
- **Peripherals:** Dual 27-inch 4K Monitors (USB-C Hub enabled), ergonomic keyboard and mouse, noise-canceling headset.

### 2. General Business & Operations Track
- **Laptop:** MacBook Air 15-inch (M3, 16GB RAM, 512GB SSD) or Lenovo ThinkPad T14 (16GB RAM, 512GB SSD, Windows 11 Pro).
- **Peripherals:** Single 27-inch QHD Monitor, standard wireless keyboard and mouse, USB-C dock.

## Hardware Refresh Cycles
- **Laptops:** Eligible for standard lifecycle refresh every **36 months (3 years)** from the date of initial assignment.
- **Monitors & Docks:** Eligible for refresh every **48 months (4 years)**.
- **Battery Replacement:** If battery health drops below 80% maximum capacity before 36 months, IT Support will service or replace the battery free of charge.

## Ordering Replacement Hardware & Peripheral Requests

### Submitting a Request
1. Hardware requests require departmental budget approval.
2. Submit a request ticket through the IT Support Agent by selecting intent **Create Support Ticket** or via internal portal `https://procure.enterprise.corp`.
3. Include your shipping address, manager approval email, and reason for replacement (e.g., hardware damage, lifecycle refresh, new role requirement).

### Damaged or Stolen Hardware Procedure
- **Accidental Damage:** Submit a ticket with photos of the damage. A loaner device will be shipped overnight while repairs are conducted.
- **Lost or Stolen Device:** Immediately contact IT Security (`security@enterprise.corp`) within 2 hours. IT will initiate a remote wipe via Jamf (Mac) or Microsoft Intune (Windows) to protect corporate intellectual property.
